Started DJing in the early 90s, when jungle and happy hardcore were underground in every sense. Vinyl ruled, pirate radio mattered, and the music came before everything else.

Cut teeth on stations like Fantasy FM and SCR, and anywhere with a sound system—youth clubs, back rooms, makeshift rigs. No stages. No ego. Just bass and pressure.

As the scene turned darker, DJing evolved into producing. Less playing tracks, more building them. Music rooted in jungle, hardcore, drum & bass, and garage—stripped back, heavy, and functional. No gloss. No filler.

That mindset now lands where it hits hardest: clubs, late nights, loud systems, dark rooms.

Underground by design.
No trends. No shine.
Just sound system music—made to be felt.
